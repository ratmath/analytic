Your own series

For a function given by its power series coefficients aₖ, the remainder after degree p needs an argument. series makes you name one and checks what it can:

  • alternating: the terms alternate in sign and shrink, so the tail lies between 0 and the first omitted term. Checked on the next 32 terms for every x in the domain.
  • ratio: |aₖ₊₁|·r ≤ q·|aₖ| with q < 1 beyond p, so the tail is at most the first omitted term divided by 1 − q. Checked on the next 32 terms.
  • lagrange: you supply M ≥ max |f⁽ᵖ⁺¹⁾| on the domain.
  • explicit: you supply the error.

What the library cannot check (the terms beyond the lookahead, your M, your error) is your claim, so the model is marked assumed, and describe() says so.
